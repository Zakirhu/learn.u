About the portal

Built for how DIPS
actually teaches

DIPS runs twelve branches with thousands of students and hundreds of teachers, all producing and needing the same kind of material. The Centralized Learning Portal gives every branch one shared library instead of a dozen separate ones — so a worksheet written in Jalandhar is just as easy to find in Begowal.
